Australia

Australia Skilled Migration Solutions

Migrating to Australia requires scoring points based on age, education, English proficiency, and work experience. We specialize in:

  • Subclass 189: Skilled Independent Visa for high-scoring applicants.
  • Subclass 190: State Nominated Visa requiring state sponsorship.
  • Subclass 491: Regional Work visa, opening pathways to Permanent Residency after 3 years.
  • Skills Assessment: ACS, VETASSESS, TRA, and CPA filing.

Our registered migration agents evaluate your credentials to select the highest-odds subclass and manage skills assessment validation.

Canada

Canada Express Entry & PNP Streams

Canada welcomes skilled workers through a merit-based Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS). We offer advisory for:

  • Express Entry: Federal Skilled Worker (FSW), Federal Skilled Trades (FST), and Canadian Experience Class (CEC).
  • Provincial Nominee Program (PNP): PNP nomination boosts your CRS score by 600 points, guaranteeing an ITA (Ontario, BC, Alberta, Saskatchewan, etc.).
  • LMIA Auditing: Legal job offer verification.

We guide clients in maximizing CRS scores (e.g. advising on French language, IELTS improvements, or PNP matching).

Family

Spouse & Child Reunification Visas

Bring your loved ones to settle with you in Australia or Canada under legal family sponsorships.

  • Spouse Sponsorship: Visa subclasses for legally married or de facto partners.
  • Child Visas: Dependent visas for biological or adopted children under eligible age brackets.
  • Parent Visas: Contributory and non-contributory categories.

Family visa documentation is highly scrutinized to prove genuine relationship criteria. Our team compiles meticulous relationship evidence history to ensure approval.

Visitor

Tourist & Business Visitor Visas

Whether visiting friends and family, attending a business summit, or going on vacation, we assist in securing visitor permits:

  • UK Standard Visitor Visas: Structured documentation proving financial ties.
  • Australia Subclass 600: Fast visa filing for tourism or stream business visits.

We ensure that travel history, assets, and tie-backs to your home country are properly demonstrated to avoid rejection.
